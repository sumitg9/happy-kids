A little about us

More than a school,
a second home.

At Happy Kids Pre-School, we believe every child is unique. Located in Sukanta Pally, Siliguri, our environment encourages children to ask questions, make friends, explore their creativity and build confidence at their own pace.

Through joyful play-based activities, caring guidance, audio-visual classes and festive celebrations, we make the early years meaningful for children and reassuring for parents.
